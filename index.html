Megawide Apps

Planners Dashboard, Procurement, Engineering and Bids in one app. Each app always opens its latest version and remembers your login.

Windows

Download .exe

Run the installer. If Windows SmartScreen shows "Windows protected your PC", choose More info → Run anyway. The app then keeps itself up to date.

Mac

Download .dmg

Open the file and drag Megawide Apps into Applications. The first time you open it, macOS says it cannot check the developer: open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway. (On older macOS, right-click the app → Open does the same.)

Phone or tablet

The Megawide Apps app is for Windows and Mac only for now. On a phone or tablet, add each app to your home screen. It opens full screen, like an app, and remembers your login:

  1. Open the app in your browser (Safari on iPhone and iPad, Chrome on Android): Planners Dashboard, Procurement, Engineering, Bids.
  2. iPhone and iPad: tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.
    Android: tap the ⋮ menu, then Add to Home screen.
